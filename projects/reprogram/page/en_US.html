The things Zowi can do are stored in its memory. In this project we are going to teach it new skills: we will turn Zowi into an anti-theft alarm and a guard robot!

The Alarm program changes Zowi's behavior: buttons A and B stop doing what they usually do, and Zowi becomes a guard that watches over and warns about movements around it. This shows how the same robot can behave in very different ways depending on the program you load into it.
