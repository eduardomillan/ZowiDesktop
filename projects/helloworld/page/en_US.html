Are you already a Zowi and Zowi app expert? Do you want to program your own games and improve Zowi? Take your first steps as a robot expert by programming Zowi with Bitbloq.

Bitbloq is a visual programming environment based on blocks that lets you create programs for Zowi without writing any code. Snap the blocks together like puzzle pieces to give your first instructions and find out what happens when you switch Zowi off and back on again.

When you have read the lesson, try the Quiz. Tap Learn more to open the full guide in your browser.
