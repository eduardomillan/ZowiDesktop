Do you think it's possible to guess things before they happen? Reprogram Zowi and ask him questions to discover what random means in this project.

The fortune-teller (Adivinawi) program lets Zowi act like a magic 8-ball. Ask him a yes-or-no question, shake him or tap his head, and he will respond with a symbol or gesture. This is a great way to explore how computers and robots generate random decisions.
