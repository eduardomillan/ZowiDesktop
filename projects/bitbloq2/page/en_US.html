Make your first smart programs! Learn to program Zowi to do different things depending on the information its sensors receive.

Sensors allow Zowi to perceive its environment. The distance sensor measures distances by emitting sound waves and calculating the time they take to return. With the conditional If... Execute block, Zowi can react differently depending on what it detects.
