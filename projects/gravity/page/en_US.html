A two-legged robot can easily fall over. Do you know what keeps Zowi upright? And what stops it toppling while it walks?

Every body has a centre of gravity: the point where all its weight seems to concentrate. Zowi's big feet lower its centre of gravity and widen the base of support, so it keeps its balance better even when it leans while moving.
