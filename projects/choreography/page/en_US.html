A choreography is a set of dance moves. Pick a song you like, invent your own choreography and get Zowi to dance to it with "3, 2, 1... Action!".

With "3, 2, 1... Action!" you chain movements together to build your own choreography. For each movement you can choose between 3 speeds and the number of repetitions; some movements also let you set the direction, so Zowi can walk forward, backward or spin. To make the dance even more fun, you can combine the movements with mouth gestures.
