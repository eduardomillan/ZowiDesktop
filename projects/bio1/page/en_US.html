You have probably noticed that Zowi realises when there are objects in front of it. But can it see them at night? Do you know how Zowi's "eyes" work? Turn off the lights and check whether it bumps into objects or avoids them just like when there is light.

Zowi's "eyes" do not see light: they are actually an ultrasonic sensor. They emit very high-pitched sounds that we cannot hear; those sounds bounce off objects and come back to the sensor like an echo. By measuring how long the echo takes to return, Zowi knows how far away things are, just like bats do.
