You may have noticed that Zowi can show expressions: it smiles, frowns or even sticks its tongue out. But do you know how it does it? It all happens in its mouth, a small panel of lights under its eyes. Do you dare to find out how it works?

Zowi's mouth is a 5×6 LED matrix (30 LEDs in total). Each LED turns on or off to form expressions: smile, heart, sad face... A mouth pattern is really a 32-bit binary number: each bit stands for one LED and tells it whether to be on or off. Zowi includes 31 predefined mouths (IDs 0 to 30), and you can create your own by drawing on the grid in the Mouth Editor.
