Robots can use different components to move around. Do you know which one Zowi uses?

Wheels are the fastest way to travel on flat ground, but Zowi does not have them: it moves with two legs driven by servos. Legs are slower than wheels, but they let the robot climb over uneven terrain and tackle obstacles, just like real animals.
