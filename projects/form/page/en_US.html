Zowi's shape is lovely, isn't it? But that is not all — the engineers also had to fit every part inside its head so the robot works. Can you guess which parts they are?

A robot needs circuits, a board, motors or servos and a battery, and all of it has to fit inside its body. Good design is not only about looks: it also leaves room for each component to slot in and work properly.

Zowi is also customisable: play around and change its look with the stickers in the box. When you have read the lesson, try the Quiz. Tap Learn more to open the full guide in your browser.
